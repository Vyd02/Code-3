Code 3

Line tracker · every truck from Station 1 to signed off

Get on the line

  1. Scan the codePoint your phone camera at it and tap the link.
  2. Create an accountUse your email and a password.
  3. Wait for your roleThe app opens once you're approved.
Can't scan? Type this in your browser:code3-line.pages.dev

Add it to your home screen

Then Code 3 opens like a regular app, with its own icon.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the link in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right
  3. Tap Add to home screen, then Install

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the link in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
